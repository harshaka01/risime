# RisiMe v0.2.0-nightly.9 — update-screen fix, emoji and reactions

Wire protocol: `contract/v1/PROTOCOL.md` **v1.8**. Server: https://risime.risicloud.ai.
**Not a required update.**

## Install (important if you were stuck on "Update required")
Install from the browser: open **https://risicloud.ai/app/risime/** on your phone, tap **Download
RisiMe**, open the file, and allow "install unknown apps" if asked. Your chats and login are kept.
Don't rely on the in-app button in older versions.

## New for users
- **The update screen is fixed:**
  - the **Update** button is always visible at the bottom, with the notes scrolling above it;
  - text is readable in dark mode;
  - notes show as plain text;
  - "Open download page" and "Sign out" are always there;
  - any download or install error shows a message with **Retry**.
- **Emoji:** an emoji button in the message box (all current emoji, including on older phones).
- **Reactions:**
  - long-press a message for 👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 🙏 or "+" for any emoji;
  - tap your reaction again to remove it;
  - counts show under the message, and tapping them shows who reacted.
  - In encrypted chats, reactions are end-to-end encrypted too.
- Message length counts characters as you see them (a family emoji counts as 1).

## Fixed
- **P0:** testers were locked out by the nightly.8 "Update required" screen. The button was off
  screen, the text was invisible in dark mode, and the notes showed raw Markdown. Mitigated by
  unsetting `required`, and fixed here.
  - Rule: the app never blocks use without a working, reachable action.
  - UI tests cover long notes, a small screen, large text and dark mode.

- Live interop, including the plaintext reaction and grapheme checks.

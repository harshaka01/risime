# RisiMe v0.2.0-nightly.15 — photo progress and retry, scrolling verified on a device

Wire protocol: `contract/v1/PROTOCOL.md` v1.13. Server: https://risime.risicloud.ai. **Not a
required update.**

## Important: update, never uninstall
Always update over the old app (Settings → Update, or https://risicloud.ai/app/risime/ in your
phone's browser). Never uninstall.

## New for users
- **Photos:**
  - Sending a photo shows "Encrypting photo…" and then the upload percentage on the photo.
  - If sending fails: "Couldn't send photo · Retry". Tap Retry to try again at once (it also
    retries by itself).
  - A retry never sends the photo twice.
  - Works the same in groups and 1:1.
- **Scrolling:** every chat (1:1 and group) opens at the newest message every time, including after
  rejoining a group, restoring history, rotating the phone or opening the keyboard. Checked on real
  Android, not only in simulations.

## Verification
- Gates: server, Android (472 tests), crypto.
- Live interop: all checks, including group photos (upload 0→100 %, a retry after a lost reply gets
  the same photo back, members decrypt) and the delete checks.
- Real Android 14 (Redroid): 11 scrolling tests on 240 messages of mixed heights (replay, restore,
  rejoin, real keyboard, rotation, "New messages ↓"). To check that the tests can fail, the
  stick-to-bottom rule was switched off on purpose: 6 of the 11 then failed.
- Automatic upgrade gate on spark2 (Redroid): the published nightly.14 is upgraded in place to this
  build, with messages kept, no sign-in, and new messages delivered.

# RisiMe v0.2.0-nightly.13 — encrypted photos, chats that stay at the bottom

Wire protocol: `contract/v1/PROTOCOL.md` v1.12 (v1.11 photos in the app; v1.12 delete is on the server
only, and the app gets it in a later release). Server: https://risime.risicloud.ai. **Not a
required update.**

## Important: update, never uninstall
Always update over the old app (Settings → Update, or https://risicloud.ai/app/risime/ in your
phone's browser). Never uninstall.

## New for users
- **Photos, end-to-end encrypted** (in encrypted chats and groups):
  - Tap the attach button, pick a photo, add a caption if you like, and send.
  - Before sending, the photo is resized (longest side 2048 px), its location and camera details
    are removed, and it's encrypted on your phone. The server only stores the encrypted file, for
    30 days.
  - A small preview shows first. Tap it to download and open the photo full screen (pinch to zoom),
    then **Save** to your gallery.
  - On Wi-Fi, photos download by themselves. On mobile data, they download only in the chat you
    have open; with Data Saver or roaming, only when you tap.
  - In a chat that isn't encrypted yet, photos are off. The app says who needs to update.
- **Chats stay at the newest message:** a chat opens at the bottom and follows new messages
  (incoming, your own sends, a sync, the keyboard opening). If you scroll up to read, it stays put
  and shows **New messages ↓** with a count.

## Verification
- Gates: server, Android, crypto.
- Live interop: all checks, plus photos in an encrypted DM and in a group. The preview comes first,
  the full photo decrypts with a SHA-256 match, interrupted downloads resume, a removed member can
  fetch an old photo but not a new one, and a non-member is refused.
- Real Android decoders (Redroid, Android 14 arm64): no GPS, EXIF, XMP, ICC or MPF in sent photos
  or previews; orientation is applied to the pixels; colours are converted to sRGB.
- Automatic upgrade gate on spark2 (Redroid): the published nightly.12 is upgraded in place to this
  build, with messages kept, no sign-in, and new messages delivered.

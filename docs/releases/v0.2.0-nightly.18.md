# RisiMe v0.2.0-nightly.18 — Delete for everyone, call hardening

Wire protocol: `contract/v1/PROTOCOL.md` v1.15 (v1.14 member re-add and v1.15 history sharing are on
the server; the app gets them in the next releases). Server: https://risime.risicloud.ai. **Not a
required update.**

## Important: update, never uninstall
Always update **over** the old app. Never uninstall or clear the app's data: that makes your phone a
new device.

## New for users
- **Delete:**
  - Long-press a message → **Delete for me**, or **Delete for everyone** (your own messages within
    48 hours; group admins can delete any message in their group).
  - Everyone sees "This message was deleted", and the server removes its copies, including photos.
  - If someone in the chat is on an older version, the app warns that they may still see it.
  - **Select** several messages to delete them at once.
  - In the chat list: **Clear chat** (empties it, keeps the chat) and **Delete chat** (removes it
    from your list).
- **Calls:** more hardening after the nightly.17 fix:
  - a caller whose app crashes mid-ring no longer leaves the other phone stuck;
  - clearer "can't take calls yet" messages.

## Verification
- Gates: server, Android, crypto.
- Live interop: delete checks D0–D8 (DM, group, admin, photo blob removed, reactions, delete for
  me).
- **Stronger automatic upgrade gate on spark2:** two Redroid phones. The published nightly.17 is
  upgraded in place to this build. Then it checks that messages are kept, the device id is kept,
  there's no sign-in, the **group survives** and works both ways, **a photo sends**, **reactions**
  work, and **delete for everyone** leaves the deleted-message line on both phones.

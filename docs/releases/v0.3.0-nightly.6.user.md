# RisiMe 0.3.0-nightly.6

## New
- Swipe right on any message to reply to it.
- Links you send now show a preview card with the page title and picture. You can turn this off in Settings → Chats.
- Better recognition of Sinhala and Tamil speech when Risi transcribes an Official call.

## Fixed
- Official chats no longer get stuck on "Starting Official…". If something goes wrong you now see why, with a Try again button.
- After a reinstall or a new phone, your Official chats work again without waiting for the other person to open RisiMe.
- 1:1 chats now say who you're waiting for, instead of "a group member".
- When your phone calendar isn't synced, Risi tells you so instead of saying you're free.

## Coming soon
- Risi transcribes your voice notes in Official chats, and answers voice notes you send it.
- Action items from Official calls and voice notes.
- Adding more people to a call.

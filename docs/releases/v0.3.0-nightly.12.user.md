# RisiMe 0.3.0-nightly.12

## New
- Settings → Calls → "Test incoming call": your phone rings in 5 seconds, so you can check calls ring.
- Translate voice notes and messages into more languages, with your recent choices at the top.
- Clearer English call transcripts: fewer words cut in half.

## Fixed
- Incoming RisiMe calls ring again after an earlier call didn't end properly.
- After a normal phone call, RisiMe no longer says you're already in a call.
- Translate shows up without opening Settings first.
- If call sounds are muted on your phone, RisiMe shows it with a Fix button.

## Coming soon
- Calling Risi, with your English speech understood on your phone itself.

# RisiMe v0.2.0-nightly.16 — 1:1 voice calls (first version)

Wire protocol: `contract/v1/PROTOCOL.md` v1.13. Server: https://risime.risicloud.ai. **Not a
required update.**

## Important: update, never uninstall
Always update over the old app (Settings → Update, or https://risicloud.ai/app/risime/ in your
phone's browser). Never uninstall.

## New for users: voice calls (1:1)
- **Making and answering calls:** in an encrypted 1:1 chat, tap the 📞 in the header. The other
  person's phones all ring; the first one to answer takes the call.
- **End-to-end encrypted:** the call setup goes through the chat's encryption. The audio is
  encrypted directly between the two phones, and the server never hears it.
- **Ringing:** works with the app closed or the phone locked. While RisiMe is locked, it rings as
  "Incoming RisiMe call" without the caller's name (decision 051); unlock to see who it is and
  answer.
- **Audio:** earpiece, speaker or Bluetooth; mute; the screen turns off near your ear. Missed and
  declined calls show as lines in the chat.
- **Who can call:** both people need this version. Otherwise the app says "<name> needs to update
  RisiMe to take calls". In a chat that isn't encrypted yet, calls are off.

## Known limits (please read)
- **Calls only connect when the two phones can reach each other directly**, for example on the
  same Wi-Fi. The relay server (TURN) on spark2 isn't reachable from the internet yet (decision
  046: the call ports are still blocked). Until then, calls between different networks or over
  mobile data will usually fail with "Can't connect the call" within a few seconds.
- Calls aren't available on older 32-bit phones; Settings → Calls says so.
- Group calls come later.

## Please test
1. Two phones on the **same Wi-Fi**: call, answer, talk, speaker, Bluetooth, hang up.
2. Callee phone **locked**, then **app closed**: it must ring, then unlock and answer.
3. Two phones on one account: both ring; answering on one stops the other.
4. Decline, and miss a call (let it ring out): the chat lines.
5. Android 14+: if it doesn't ring full screen, allow "full-screen notifications" for RisiMe.
6. **Mobile data:** expect "Can't connect the call" quickly until the ports are opened. It must
   never hang.

- Live interop: 10 call checks (ringing on both devices, first answer wins, SDP rules, trickled

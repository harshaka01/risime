# RisiMe v0.2.0-nightly.22 — 1:1 chats repair themselves after a reinstall

Wire protocol: `contract/v1/PROTOCOL.md` v1.16. Server: https://risime.risicloud.ai. **Not a
required update.**

## Important: update, never uninstall
Always update **over** the old app. Never uninstall or clear the app's data.

## Fixed (P0)
- **1:1 chats where nothing could be sent after a reinstall:**
  - A reinstalled phone is a new device, and nothing added it to its existing 1:1 encryption
    groups. Messages in those chats stayed unsent ("Not sent — tap to retry").
  - Now the other person's phone (or your own other phone) adds the new phone and drops the old
    installs automatically.
  - If nobody can (for example both of you reinstalled), your app resets that chat's encryption by
    itself. Older messages can then be fetched with **Request history**.
  - Meanwhile the chat shows "Setting up encryption on this phone…".
- After the release, a one-off repair runs for the chats already affected on the pilot.

## Verification
- Gates: server (491 tests), Android (633 tests).
- Live interop: A reinstalls; B's phone re-adds A's new phone and drops the old one; messages flow
  both ways. Both reinstall, then the reset fallback. A gate check that a DM still sends both ways
  after a device change.
- Automatic two-phone upgrade gate on spark2 (Redroid): the published nightly.21 is upgraded in
  place to this build.

# RisiMe v0.2.0-nightly.12 — groups come back after logout

Wire protocol: `contract/v1/PROTOCOL.md` v1.11 (no wire change in this release). Server:
https://risime.risicloud.ai. **Not a required update.**

## Important: update, never uninstall
Always update over the old app (Settings → Update, or https://risicloud.ai/app/risime/ in your
phone's browser). Never uninstall.

## Fixed
- **Groups after logout/login (P0):**
  - A group no longer turns into "New group" with its messages gone and sending broken.
  - After you sign in again, the app rejoins every group automatically: another member's phone
    re-adds yours.
  - Until then the group shows its known name (or "Rejoining group…"), and the message box says
    "Rejoining… you can send once this phone is back in the group". Messages you type are sent once
    you're back in.
  - Messages that can't be decrypted on the new sign-in show one line instead of gaps.
  - If you're the group's only admin and you logged out, the group is rebuilt; its name may show as
    "Group" until someone renames it.
- **After updating from nightly.10:** the app no longer fetches the whole inbox again on every
  reconnect, and notifications aren't muted after the one-time recovery.

## Verification
- Gates: server, Android, crypto.
- Live interop: all checks, plus the new group rejoin checks. B logs out while the only admin is
  offline, logs in again, and is re-added; the right name and history line show, and messages flow
  both ways.
- **Upgrade gate, automatic on spark2 (Redroid, decision 045):** the published nightly.11, signed in
  with chats, is upgraded in place to this build. Every message is kept, no sign-in is asked, and a
  new message after the upgrade arrives.

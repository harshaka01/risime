# RisiMe v0.2.0-nightly.5 — invites and friends

Wire protocol: `contract/v1/PROTOCOL.md` **v1.6**. Server: https://risime.risicloud.ai.

## New
- **Invite-only sign-up:**
  - Invite someone by phone, email and name, then share the invite (link plus "sign in with
    <email>") from your phone.
  - When they sign in with that email through RisiCloud, they get an account with that phone,
    shown as **"vouched by <you>"**, and you become friends automatically.
  - The admin allowlist still works as a second way in.
- **Friends:**
  - Add a friend by phone number. The reply is always "Request sent", whether or not the number is
    on RisiMe, and you can invite them instead.
  - A **Requests** tab with a badge: Accept, Decline or Block incoming requests, and cancel your
    own.
  - **Only friends can chat**, type to each other or see each other's presence. Your friend list is
    private.
  - Unfriend, block and unblock.
- Existing conversations became friendships automatically (Harsha ↔ Shenika), so no chat broke.
- **Updater:** an update banner with the release notes, one-tap install, and on Android 12+ a
  silent update where the system allows it (decision 027).
- Server side, dormant until Harsha's Firebase keys exist: push device registry and data-only
  wake-ups (contract v1.5, decisions 026/028).

## Reliability
The pilot runs as a systemd service with auto-restart, a 5-minute health check, backups before
every deploy and one-command rollback (decision 025).

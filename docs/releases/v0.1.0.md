# RisiMe v0.1.0 — two people chat one-to-one

Released 2026-10-06 from `main` at the `v0.1.0` tag. Wire protocol: `contract/v1/PROTOCOL.md` **v1.1**.

## What's in it
- Phone + email-code login (allowlist only), with dev OTP logging.
- Contacts: everyone on the allowlist; people who haven't logged in yet are greyed out.
- Real-time one-to-one text chat with ✓ / ✓✓ / read ticks.
- Store-and-forward: messages that arrive while the app is closed are delivered when it reopens.
- An outbox that survives going offline: messages stay pending and are resent once, with no
  duplicates.
- A persistent "Dev build — not end-to-end encrypted" banner.
- A local-only behaviour log on Android (never uploaded).

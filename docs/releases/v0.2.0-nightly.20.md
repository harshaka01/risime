# RisiMe v0.2.0-nightly.20 — chat history comes back on a new phone

Wire protocol: `contract/v1/PROTOCOL.md` v1.15. Server: https://risime.risicloud.ai. **Not a
required update.**

## Important: update, never uninstall
Always update **over** the old app. Never uninstall or clear the app's data.

## New for users: history sharing (decision 049)
- **On a new phone,** or after "Log out and delete chats", or after a reinstall, encrypted chats
  show "Earlier messages aren't available on this device" → tap **Request history**.
- **Your own other phone** asks once: "Your new phone wants your chat history. Allow?" After that
  it shares automatically with that phone. The old phone must be unlocked and open RisiMe to share.
- **In groups, if you have no other phone,** a member is asked: "<name> wants the group history from
  when they were in the group. Share?" Only messages from the time you were a member are shared,
  and they're labelled **"Shared by <name>"**.
- The history is encrypted for your new phone only. Deleted messages never come back. Settings →
  Privacy shows your approvals.

## Known limits
- Reactions confirmed before this version aren't shared.
- The approvals list shows a short device code, not the phone's name.
- A member's request shows as a dialog and a notification, not inside the chat.

## Verification
- Gates: server (482 tests), Android (617 tests), crypto (vectors cross-checked by an independent
  Python HPKE implementation).
- Live interop: an own new phone gets history after one Allow, and later automatically; the member
  path shares only the membership period, labelled; deleted messages are never re-imported.
- Automatic two-phone upgrade gate on spark2 (Redroid): the published nightly.19 is upgraded in
  place to this build. Messages, the device id, the group, photos, reactions and delete for

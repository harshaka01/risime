# RisiMe v0.2.0-nightly.11 — hotfix: chats kept on logout paths, notifications, reinstall history

Wire protocol: `contract/v1/PROTOCOL.md` **v1.11** (v1.10 implemented; v1.11 images arrive in
nightly.12). Server: https://risime.risicloud.ai. **Not a required update.**

## Important: update, never uninstall
Your chat history lives only on your phone until encrypted backup ships. **Always update over the
old app** (Settings → Update, or install the APK from https://risicloud.ai/app/risime/ over the
existing app). Uninstalling or clearing the app's data loses local history: encrypted messages from
before the reinstall can't be read on the new install.

## Hotfix (P0 after nightly.10: chats disappeared)
- **Your chats are safe:**
  - Logging out now always asks you to confirm first. No other path ever deletes your chats.
  - A sign-in problem shows **Sign in again** and keeps everything.
  - In nightly.10, "Log out" (and the Sign out buttons on the lock, update and phone screens)
    deleted all chats with one tap and no question.
- **Messages that disappeared come back:**
  - After updating, the app fetches your inbox again once, so non-encrypted messages from the last
    30 days return: those you received, and your own sent ones.
  - No encrypted messages or groups were affected.
- **Groups:**
  - Friends can be ticked in **New group** again. The whole row is tappable, and a greyed-out
    friend tells you why (they need to update).
  - The server no longer treats a friend as "needs to update" because of an old copy of the app.
- **Chats** open at the newest message and follow new ones. If you've scrolled up, a
  **New messages ↓** button takes you down.
- **Code screens** show a countdown before you can resend, so no tap can lock you out.

## New for users
- **Notifications:** new messages wake the app even when it's closed. The wake-up carries no sender
  and no text; the app fetches the message itself and shows the notification from your phone.
  Allow notifications when Android asks.
- **Reinstall / new phone:**
  - Your own sent messages in non-encrypted chats come back after a reinstall (last 30 days), not
    only the other person's.
  - In encrypted chats, messages from before this install can't be decrypted on it by design. The
    chat now says so in one line, "Earlier messages aren't available on this device", instead of
    silently missing them.
  - If a message ever can't be decrypted, the chat shows "Some messages couldn't be decrypted"
    instead of dropping it silently.
  - Restored messages keep their original dates, and a reinstall doesn't flood you with old
    notifications.

## Fixed
- After a reinstall a chat showed only the other person's messages (decision 043).
- Encryption setup ran while the app was still locked (fingerprint), failed, and left encryption
  and groups off until the next restart.

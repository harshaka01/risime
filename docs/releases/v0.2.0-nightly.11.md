# RisiMe v0.2.0-nightly.11 — notifications, reinstall history

Wire protocol: `contract/v1/PROTOCOL.md` **v1.11** (v1.10 implemented; v1.11 images arrive in
nightly.12). Server: https://risime.risicloud.ai. **Not a required update.**

## Important: update, never uninstall
Your chat history lives only on your phone until encrypted backup ships. **Always update over the
old app** (Settings → Update, or install the APK from https://risicloud.ai/app/risime/ over the
existing app). Uninstalling or clearing the app's data loses local history: encrypted messages from
before the reinstall can't be read on the new install.

## New for users
- **Notifications:** new messages wake the app even when it's closed. The wake-up carries no sender
  and no text; the app fetches the message itself and shows the notification from your phone.
  Allow notifications when Android asks.
- **Reinstall / new phone:**
  - Your own sent messages in non-encrypted chats come back after a reinstall (last 30 days), not
    only the other person's.
  - In encrypted chats, messages from before this install can't be decrypted on it by design. The
    chat now says so in one line, "Earlier messages aren't available on this device", instead of
    silently missing them.
  - If a message ever can't be decrypted, the chat shows "Some messages couldn't be decrypted"
    instead of dropping it silently.
  - Restored messages keep their original dates, and a reinstall doesn't flood you with old
    notifications.
